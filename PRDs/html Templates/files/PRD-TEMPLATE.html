Purpose

State in two or three sentences what this process is for and what changes when it works. Write it so a reader who has never seen the system can tell whether the requirements that follow actually serve the purpose.

Client Intake (MN-INTAKE) captures a prospective mentee from first contact through to the point where the mentee is eligible to be matched with a mentor. It ends where Mentor Matching (MN-MATCH) begins.

Scope

In scope

  • Capture of a prospective mentee's contact and business details.
  • Eligibility screening against the published criteria.
  • Assignment of an intake owner and the record of that assignment.

Out of scope

  • Mentor recruitment and vetting, covered by Mentor Onboarding (MR-ONBOARD).
  • Any matching logic, covered by Mentor Matching (MN-MATCH).
Standard

Level 1 and Level 2 documents name no products. Describe the capability required, not the system that provides it. Product names belong in implementation documentation only.

Definitions

Prospective mentee
A business owner who has expressed interest but has not yet been screened.
Intake owner
The staff member accountable for completing screening for one prospective mentee.

Actors

ActorRole in this processWhat they must be able to do
Prospective mentee Originates the record Submit contact and business details without an account, and receive confirmation that the submission was received.
Intake owner Screens and progresses

See every prospective mentee assigned to them, ordered by how long the record has been waiting.

Record a screening outcome and the reason for it.
Program director Oversees See intake volume and time-to-screen across all owners without opening individual records.

Data requirements

The table below is the reason this document is HTML rather than Markdown: grouped column headers, controlled widths, and cells that hold more than one line.

Fields captured during Client Intake (MN-INTAKE)
Constraints
FieldTypeRequiredUniqueNotes
Business name Text Yes No Two applicants may legitimately share a business name. Duplicate detection is a review prompt, never a block.
Primary contact email Email Yes Yes

Uniqueness is enforced across all prospective mentees, not only open ones.

A repeat submission from the same address updates the existing record rather than creating a second one.
Years in operation Whole number Yes No Drives eligibility. Zero is a valid answer and must be distinguishable from an unanswered field.
Areas of need Multi-select Yes No At least one required. Values:
  • Financial management
  • Marketing and sales
  • Operations
  • Staffing
Intake owner Staff reference No No Empty until assignment. Becomes required before a screening outcome can be recorded.

Process

StepActionActorOutcome and exit condition
1 Submit enquiry Prospective mentee Record created with status Received. Confirmation sent to the submitted address.
2 Assign owner Program director Intake owner set. Status moves to In screening.
3 Screen against criteria Intake owner

Outcome recorded as Eligible, Not eligible, or Deferred, with a reason in every case.

Eligible records exit this process into Mentor Matching (MN-MATCH).
