## Task

Build Group (Ctrl+G) and Ungroup (Ctrl+Shift+G) of General UI PRD Section 3.6 (`PRDs/SnapMock-General-UI-PRD.html`, version 2.5 at the start of this work; the work bumps it to 2.6). The table says: Group "combines selected items into a single group that moves, resizes, and transforms as one unit", requires two or more items selected; Ungroup "dissolves selected group(s) back into individual items, restoring their original positions and properties", requires a group selected. Both rows exist today and explain the deferral (`MainWindow._arrange_group`, `_arrange_ungroup`, `_GROUP_DEFERRAL`); the work replaces the message with the behaviour.

A group is a new item type, which the item hierarchy of Technical Architecture PRD Section 4.2 does not have, so the work also covers everything a new item type touches: the item registry and `items.json`, the clipboard, Duplicate, z-order, layer membership, locking, the Select tool's hit testing and transforms, the Property Panel, the Layer Panel thumbnails, the walks over the scene's items, the four exports, the Snagit writer, and the Technical Architecture PRD (Section 4.2, the `.smk` format table of Section 3.8, which the PRD's own change-log rows call Section 6.1, and Section 10). Where a walk over the scene's items exists today, the work decides once what a group means to it and says so.

The session opens by presenting the two decisions below with the consequential decision template and waits. Nothing is built before they are taken.

## Decisions to surface

Apply the two-part test from the global guidance. Two decisions are expected to pass it; present both with the consequential decision template before step 2 and wait.

- **1. What a group is.** Option A, a container item: `GroupItem` is a `SnapGraphicsItem` whose members are its Qt child items; the group owns the layer membership, the z-value, the lock, and the transform; a click on a member resolves to the group; `items.json` nests the members inside the group's entry; Ungroup re-parents the members to the scene with the group's transform composed into each. It is one unit in every sense the PRD row uses, nests naturally, and rides the existing single-item transform path; its cost is that every walk over `scene.items()` now meets child items and must choose top-level or all (step 5), and that a member cannot be edited in place without ungrouping. Option B, a membership tag: a `group_id` on `SnapGraphicsItem`, serialized as one field, no new item type; the Select tool expands a click on a member to every item with the same tag, the handles frame the set, and the existing multi-item transform path moves and resizes them; Ungroup clears the tag. It touches no walk and no hierarchy, and "restoring their original positions and properties" is true by construction; its cost is that nothing is one unit, so every selection path (rubber band, Tab, Select All, Duplicate, Move to Layer, Delete, the context menu, the Property Panel's mixed indicators) has to expand the tag one by one and every future feature has to remember to, nesting needs a parent chain, and the Technical Architecture's hierarchy and `SelectionGroup` name still need a correction. Recommendation: A; the cost is step 5.
- **2. A group whose members sit on different layers.** Option A, refuse: Group requires every selected item on one layer, and the Section 1.3 message names it ("Group needs the selected items on one layer"). Option B, gather: the group lands on the active layer, or on the topmost member's layer, and the members leave theirs. A keeps the layer model exact (an item belongs to one layer, and so does a group); B is what a user who selected across layers probably meant, at the cost of silently moving items between layers inside one command. Recommendation: A; the cost is one more message the user can meet.

Everything else follows the PRD; where the PRD is silent, decide, note it under the notes' Section 17 and the PRD 2.6 rows, and continue. Six silences are known:

- What double-click on a group does: nothing (text editing needs Ungroup first), or enters the group to edit a text member. Recommended: nothing, with the status hint saying so.
- What the Property Panel's Appearance section does for a selected group: hidden (Transform and Item Info only), or the multi-selection controls of Section 8.6 applied to the vector members as one command. Recommended: the Section 8.6 controls over the members, since that path exists.
- Whether `format_version` in `manifest.json` stays 1 when `items.json` carries nested entries: an older build skips the unknown type and loses the group's members. Recommended: stay at 1 and record in the Technical Architecture format table (Section 3.8, "Section 6.1" in its rows) that a group entry is opaque to earlier builds; every build so far is a development build.
- What the Snagit writer does with a group: writes the members as individual items (Snagit files have no group in the mapping the reader knows) with no warning, or skips the group with a warning. Recommended: the members individually.
- Whether a locked group's members can be selected: no; the group's lock is the members' lock, as a layer's is.
- Whether the Library panel or the Layer Panel show anything group-specific: no; a group is an item like any other to both.
